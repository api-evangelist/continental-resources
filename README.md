# Continental Resources (continental-resources)

Continental Resources is a privately held independent oil and natural gas exploration and production company headquartered in Oklahoma City with operations focused on the Bakken play of North Dakota and Montana and the SCOOP and STACK plays of Oklahoma. The company was taken private in November 2022 in a $4.3B transaction with the Hamm family and is no longer publicly traded. Continental Resources does not publish public developer APIs; this profile catalogs its corporate, investor, sustainability, and operational web properties.
